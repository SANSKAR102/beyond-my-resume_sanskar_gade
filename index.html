06 — VOICE-POWERED BUILD STORY

Built with Voice, Driven by Ideas

This portfolio started with a simple workflow: I spoke about the experience I wanted, then used AI-assisted tools to turn those ideas into a website. The process taught me to explain, review and improve.

♩

IDEA → SPOKEN → STRUCTURED → BUILT

↓
MY ORIGINAL IDEA

I wanted a unique, interactive portfolio that represents my personality, learning journey and projects — instead of making a traditional resume website.

WHY I CHOSE VOICE

I wanted to express my ideas naturally using Wispr Flow and turn my thoughts into clear website instructions.

WHAT I BUILT USING VOICE

The original portfolio's structure, content and interactions were shaped through voice-first prompting with Wispr Flow and Emergent.

CHALLENGES I FACED

At first, I was unsure where to start. Understanding the tools, explaining my ideas clearly and improving the generated website took patience and several attempts.

WHAT I LEARNED

I learned that I don't need to know everything before starting. Experimenting, asking better questions and improving step by step can turn an idea into something real.

THE VALUE OF VOICE

Speaking helped me get my thoughts out naturally. It made describing an idea feel more direct, especially when I was still figuring out exactly what I wanted.

01

Wispr Flow

I speak naturally and use voice input to express the idea without needing to type every thought.

02

AI-assisted building

I turn those thoughts into instructions, review the output and refine the result until it is closer to the idea.
